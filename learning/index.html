Learning Material

The Beginner course runs over eight classes. Each class has a page here. The page tells you what a learner is able to do by the end of it.

If you arrived by scanning a code in the book, you are in the right place. Pick the chapter you are reading.

These pages list outcomes and how we teach. They do not carry the course itself. The lessons, exercises and answers stay in the classroom and in the book.

The eight classes

Chapter 1 What is AI? What AI is, what it is not, and where it already sits in an ordinary day. Chapter 2 Prompt Writing How to ask a machine for something and get back an answer you can use. Chapter 3 How Machines Learn Train a model yourself. Then see what it gets right, and what it gets wrong. Chapter 4 Breadboard Fundamentals Build a working circuit with your hands, and find out what makes it stop working. Chapter 5 The Arduino and AI as Your Coder Read code you did not write, ask AI to change it, and test the change on real hardware. Chapter 6 Building With Blocks Make a program by dragging blocks, then use it to switch a real light on and off. Chapter 7 Speak, See, Create Speak instead of typing, make pictures from words, and ask a machine what is in a photograph. Chapter 8 Describe It, Build It, Own It Describe the software you want in plain words, build it, and be able to explain every part of it.

How we teach

  • One core concept per class. Eight classes, eight ideas. Nothing is covered twice and nothing is rushed.
  • Hands on before theory. You type a prompt in the first hour, before anyone explains what a prompt is. The explanation lands better afterwards.
  • Practice in company. Weekend classes are followed by weekday practice slots in the lab, with someone there to help.
  • Mixed ages at one table. The youngest in a batch may be ten and the oldest past fifty. Each takes something from the other.
  • Careful use throughout. Knowing when not to trust an answer runs through all eight classes rather than sitting in one.

Ready to join a batch?

The Beginner course is running now. No entrance test, no age limit, and no prior computer knowledge needed.
