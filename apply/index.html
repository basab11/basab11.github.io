Apply

The Beginner course is running now. Batches are fifteen learners, so seats are limited.

Who can join

Anyone. There is no entrance test, no age limit, and no prior computer knowledge needed. A Class 5 student and a first-time computer user in their fifties can walk in on the same day and both find a starting point. That is not a slogan. It is how the batches are actually made up.

What it costs

A small fee is charged. It goes towards keeping the lab running, the machines maintained and the parts stocked. No volunteer takes anything from it. Ask us for the current amount and we will tell you.

Where you will be coming

Bharat Sevashram Sangha, Sriniwaspuri, New Delhi.

Drag the map to look around, or open it in Google Maps for directions.

What you are committing to

  • Weekend classes 8 × 3 hours
  • Weekday practice 13 × 2 hours
  • Runs over About 4 to 5 weeks
  • Where Sriniwaspuri, New Delhi

Weekend classes are Saturdays and Sundays. Weekday practice slots are booked to suit you, and you need to finish all of them to earn the certificate.

How to apply

  1. Fill in the application form. It takes about three minutes.
  2. We call you to confirm a place and tell you when the next batch starts.
  3. You pay the fee at the lab and your seat is held.

Fill in the application form

The form opens on Google Forms. It asks for your name, a phone number and a little about your background. It does not ask for any Aadhaar number, bank detail or password, and it never will.

Or just talk to us

If you would rather ask a few questions first, that is completely fine.
