Disclaimer and copyright

Written in the same plain language as the rest of this site, because a page nobody can read protects nobody.

Who runs this site

The Bharat Tech-Shakti Mission, a group of volunteers. The Swami Pranavananda AI & Robotics Lab is at Bharat Sevashram Sangha, Sriniwaspuri, New Delhi. Every volunteer works unpaid and none of them draws a salary or a benefit from the Mission.

Copyright

© 2026 Bharat Tech-Shakti Mission. The words on this site and the photographs on it belong to the Mission unless we have said otherwise.

You are welcome to quote a short passage if you say where it came from and link back to the page. Please write to us first before reproducing a whole page or any photograph.

The teaching material itself is a different matter. The chapters, the slides, the worksheets, the exercises and the assessments are not published on this site and are not free to copy. This is a paid course and that material is how it pays for itself.

Names that belong to other people

We name the tools we teach with. ChatGPT, Claude, Google, Teachable Machine, Google Forms, Google Maps, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, Scratch and Alexa are the property of the companies that own them.

We name them because a learner needs to know what to open. Naming them is not a claim of partnership, sponsorship or approval. None of those companies endorse this Mission, and we do not speak for any of them.

What the course promises, and what it does not

The outcomes listed on these pages are what each class sets out to achieve. They are not a promise of a job, an income, an exam result or a salary. What a learner walks away with depends on the work they put in, and we would rather say that plainly than imply otherwise.

Sending an application does not hold a seat. A batch is fifteen learners, so places are limited. We confirm a place by telephone.

Course length, class timings and the fee can change. We will tell you where things stand when you apply.

About AI and the answers it gives

This is the first thing the course teaches, so it belongs on this page too. AI tools can be confidently wrong. They produce an answer in the same steady tone whether that answer is right or not, and nothing inside them is checking against the world.

Nothing on this site is legal, medical or financial advice. For anything to do with your health, your money, or anything carrying a legal consequence, ask a qualified person. Do not act on what a machine told you without checking it first.

The Mission is not responsible for what anyone does with an AI tool during the course or afterwards. Teaching somebody to use a tool carefully is what we can do. The decisions remain theirs.

Pages that are not ours

The application form is a Google Form. The map is a Google Map. Our social pages sit on Facebook, Instagram and X. Those services are run by other companies under their own terms and their own privacy policies. We do not control them and we are not responsible for them.

What this website collects

This website asks you for nothing. There is no login here and no contact form here.

Two things on the site do reach other companies. The map loads from Google when you scroll down to it, and not before. The counter on the home page asks a counting service for a number. Neither of them is given your name.

If you fill in the application form, what you type goes to a private spreadsheet that only Mission volunteers can open. We use it to contact you about a seat and for nothing else. We do not sell it and we do not pass it to anybody.

We will never ask you for an Aadhaar number, a bank detail, a card number or a password. Not on this site, not on the form, and not on the telephone. If somebody says they are from the Mission and asks you for any of those, it is not us. Please tell us if that happens.

Photographs of people

The photographs on this site are used with the agreement of the people in them. If you appear in one and would like it removed, write to us and we will take it down.

Mistakes

We check what goes on this site, and we will still get something wrong sometimes. If you find an error, tell us and we will correct it.
